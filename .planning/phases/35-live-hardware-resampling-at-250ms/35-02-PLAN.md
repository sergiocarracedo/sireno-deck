# Plan 35-02: Harden Live Hardware Resampling Across Placeholder, Reload, And Reconnect Edges

<objective>
Close the honest operational gaps around Phase 35's new live hardware behavior. This slice makes the feature reliable instead of merely impressive on the happy path: startup placeholder handoff, capture failures, runtime reloads, and device reconnects must all remain truthful once the renderer is allowed to keep sending later frames to the hardware.
</objective>
