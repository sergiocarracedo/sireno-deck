# Plan 29-03: Finish The Remaining Built-In TSX And Utility Cleanup

<objective>
Finish Phase 29 by making the rest of the shipped built-ins tell the same story as the contract and date-time slices: one-button-per-file where required, TSX-first rendering everywhere, and simple built-in styling expressed through the Sireno utility layer instead of inline style escape hatches. This final slice is demoable on its own because emoji-selector and the core button family are the remaining visible built-in surfaces users interact with after date-time.
</objective>
